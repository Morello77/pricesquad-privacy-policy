# Privacy Policy for PriceSquad

**Last updated:** October 2026
**App:** PriceSquad (Android), by Morello Developers
**Contact:** morellodevelopers@gmail.com

PriceSquad helps you check market prices, plan your shopping and see what other people pay across Nigeria. You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.

## In short
- Your market list, the prices you paid and your own items **stay on your phone**.
- Prices are sent to PriceSquad **only** when you report them, or when you have **turned on sharing**. They are sent with a random anonymous ID, never with your name.
- We use **Google Firebase** to run the app's online features. We **do not sell** your data, and the app shows **no ads**.

## 1. Information that stays on your phone
These are saved only on your device and are **not sent to us**:
- Your market list, quantities and ticked items
- The prices you paid, your "Last paid" history, and your monthly savings card
- Items you added yourself ("Add to my list anyway")
- Your chosen state and city, and your sharing setting
- Saved price estimates, so the app works on a weak network

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes this information.

## 2. Information sent to our online service (Google Firebase)
**a. Anonymous ID.** When you open the app, Firebase Authentication creates a random ID for your phone. It is not linked to your name, phone number or email. We use it to run the app and to limit spam (for example, at most 3 price reports per item per day).

**b. Price reports.** When you submit a price on the report form, or record a price on My List or in the price checker **while sharing is turned on**, we receive: the item, the price, your state, your city or LGA, the area (optional), the time, and your anonymous ID. You choose the location from a list. **The app never uses your GPS or exact location.**

**c. Item requests.** When you tap "Ask us to add it", we receive the item name you typed (up to 40 characters), your state, the time and your anonymous ID. Please don't type personal information in item names.

**d. Technical information.** Like most online services, Google may process technical data (such as IP address and device type) to deliver the service securely. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Information we do NOT collect
Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise or background location, or payment details. PriceSquad has no ads and no advertising trackers.

## 4. How information is shown and shared
- **Other users** see shared price reports (item, price, location, time) and typical prices worked out from them. The app never shows your anonymous ID to other users.
- **Google Firebase** stores and processes data for us as our service provider.
- We may publish or share **anonymous, combined price statistics** (for example, the typical price of rice in Lagos this month). These never include anonymous IDs.
- **Your savings card** is made on your phone. It is shared only if you choose to share it, and it shows your state, never your city or area.
- We **do not sell** personal data.

## 5. Your choices
- **Sharing:** turn price sharing on or off at any time (gear icon on My List).
- **Reporting:** the report form is always optional.
- **Deleting:** uninstall the app, or clear its data, to remove everything stored on your phone. Shared reports are anonymous, so we usually can't tell which ones are yours. If you have a concern, email us and we will do our best to help.

## 6. How long we keep data
Price reports are kept to show price history and trends, and we may remove old reports over time. Item requests are kept until we have reviewed them.

## 7. Security
Data is sent in encrypted form, and our database rules limit what each app user can read or write.

## 8. Children
PriceSquad is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We will update this page when the app changes, and change the "Last updated" date.
