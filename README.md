# Privacy Policy for PriceSquad

**Last updated:** January 2026

PriceSquad (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

PriceSquad does **not** require users to create accounts and does **not** collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or precise location data.

The App may collect the following non-personal data:

- **App activity data**, such as price submissions and interactions within the app
- **Device or other IDs**, such as anonymous identifiers used to support app functionality and analytics
- **Crash and performance data**, used to improve app stability and reliability (if enabled)

Users manually select locations (such as city or market) from a dropdown. The App does **not** access GPS, precise location, or background location data.

---

## 2. How We Use Data

Collected data is used only to:
- Provide core app functionality
- Display community-submitted prices
- Improve app performance and user experience

---

## 3. Data Sharing

PriceSquad does **not** sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

---

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect collected data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

PriceSquad is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
